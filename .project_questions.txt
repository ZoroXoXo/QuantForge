Q)Why we used gaussian filter as a starting point?

A)
-It reduces random noise. Gaussian filtering smooths small pixel variations,
which may help us recover the overall signal trend.
-It's a standard image-processing technique. OpenCV provides cv2.GaussianBlur(), 
so it's straightforward to implement and test.
-It gives us a useful baseline. We can measure how well a simple smoothing 
technique performs before trying more specialized methods.
-It has a trade-off we can study. Stronger smoothing reduces noise but can 
also blur sharp changes in the signal.

our noise is added to the numerical signal before it is drawn, so it isn't 
ordinary pixel noise. Gaussian filtering may smooth the rendered line, but
rasterization and line thickness also affect the result.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q)Why do you think Gaussian filtering gives the center pixel more weight than 
pixels farther away?

A)Because the center pixel is the pixel we're currently processing, so it's 
usually the most relevant to its new value. Nearby pixels are also relevant,
but pixels farther away are less likely to represent the same local detail.

Giving the center more weight helps reduce noise without changing the original 
pixel value too aggressively.
