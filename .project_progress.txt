#Applying image filtering

we will use gaussian filtering

Gaussian filtering smooths an image by averaging neighboring pixels,
 giving more weight to pixels that are closer to the pixel being processed.
This helps reduce small variations, but it can also blur important details.

how it is implemented in this project:

    -Convert the noisy numerical signal into a grayscale image.
    -Apply Gaussian filtering to that image.
    -Recover or estimate the signal from the filtered image.
    -Compare the result against the known clean synthetic signal.
